**Approach 1: Industrial Editorial**

**Design Movement:** Brutalist editorial meets enterprise tech — think Monocle magazine crossed with a Bloomberg Terminal

**Core Principles:**
- Raw typographic power: headlines dominate the page, text IS the design
- Structured asymmetry: content blocks offset by 1/3 grid, never centered
- Monochromatic restraint with one surgical accent color
- Information density that signals expertise, not simplicity

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black (#0D0D0D) backgrounds for hero sections, warm off-white (#F5F0E8) for content sections. A single electric accent — the brand's lime green (#C8FF00) used only for critical CTAs and data callouts. The restraint makes every use of color feel intentional and urgent.

**Layout Paradigm:** Newspaper-style column grids. Hero uses a 7/5 split — massive headline left, data callout right. Sections alternate between full-bleed dark and warm-white. No centered hero layouts.

**Signature Elements:**
- Oversized running numbers (01, 02, 03) as section anchors
- Horizontal rule dividers with label text (like magazine section breaks)
- Monospaced data readouts for stats/metrics

**Interaction Philosophy:** Minimal animation — content slides in from left on scroll, no bouncing or floating. Hover states reveal underlining on links, not color changes. Feels like a precision instrument.

**Animation:** Staggered text reveal on scroll (lines appear sequentially). Section transitions use a horizontal wipe. No parallax.

**Typography System:** Display: Bebas Neue (ultra-condensed, all-caps for section headers). Body: DM Sans (clean, readable). Accent labels: Space Mono (monospaced for technical credibility).

**Approach 2: Executive Editorial (SELECTED)**

**Design Movement:** Premium B2B editorial — think Harvard Business Review meets Linear.app — authoritative, warm, and architecturally confident

**Core Principles:**
- Editorial hierarchy: every section tells a chapter of the story
- Warm restraint: cream and charcoal palette signals executive-level trust
- Typographic drama: ultra-bold display headers contrast with refined body text
- Platform credibility through structural precision, not decoration

**Color Philosophy:** Primary canvas is warm cream (#F7F3EC) — approachable yet premium. Deep charcoal (#111111) for dark hero sections and emphasis. Warm amber/gold (#D4A853) as the accent — replacing neon green for executive contexts. The brand's lime green is preserved as a secondary accent for technical elements only. This palette says "we are serious operators, not a startup."

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial grid. Hero: full-bleed dark section with left-aligned oversized headline. Content sections alternate between cream and charcoal backgrounds. Case studies presented as editorial list items (like a magazine table of contents). Services in a horizontal card scroll.

**Signature Elements:**
- Full-bleed color section transitions (cream → charcoal → cream)
- Editorial case study list with client name + description + arrow link
- Oversized pull-quote typography for testimonials

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth, confident scroll animations. Cards lift slightly on hover. Logo tickers scroll continuously. CTAs use underline-on-hover, not color flash.

**Animation:** Fade-up entrance for text blocks (subtle, 0.4s). Continuous horizontal ticker for logos. Smooth section color transitions. No aggressive animations.

**Typography System:** Display: Barlow Condensed Bold / Extra Bold (editorial, impactful). Body: Inter (clean enterprise standard). Labels: Inter Medium with wide letter-spacing.

**Design Movement:** Swiss International Style meets SaaS — Stripe-level precision with B2B consulting gravitas

**Core Principles:**
- Grid absolutism: 12-column grid, every element snaps to it

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rules between every section
- Data tables styled as editorial infographics
- Minimal icon set (line icons only, 1px stroke)

**Interaction Philosophy:** Zero animation except for essential transitions. Hover states are purely typographic (weight change, not color). Feels like a precision tool for serious operators.

**Animation:** Page load fade-in only. No scroll animations. Transitions are instant or 150ms maximum.

**Typography System:** Display: GT Super (editorial serif for headlines — signals authority). Body: Inter. This is the only approach using a serif, which creates instant editorial differentiation.

## Selected Approach: Executive Editorial (Approach 2)

This approach best serves the CMO/GTM ICP because it combines editorial warmth with technical authority. The warm cream/charcoal palette signals executive-level trust while the oversized typography creates the dramatic visual impact seen in the inspiration page. The amber accent replaces neon green in executive contexts while preserving brand recognition.
